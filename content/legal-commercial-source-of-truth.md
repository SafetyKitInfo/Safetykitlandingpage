## Status key

- **Verified** — supported by the live product, configuration, executed agreement or responsible technical owner.
- **Decision required** — a commercial or legal decision has not been made.
- **Technical verification required** — an engineer or provider configuration must confirm it.
- **Legal review required** — proposed wording requires Australian legal review.
- **Not offered** — must not appear in public copy.

## 3. Confirmed product claims

The following statements are approved for current public copy:

- SafetySight helps teams record first-aid kit checks.
- Users can track expiry dates entered by their team.
- Users can keep a history of checks and follow-up actions.
- SafetySight supports organisation-based account setup.
- SightScan is a kit-scanning workflow with plan-based usage allowances.
- SafetySight supports rather than certifies a customer's workplace process.
- SafetySight does not provide legal advice or certify WHS compliance.
- Automated or entered results should be reviewed by a person where they affect workplace-safety decisions.
